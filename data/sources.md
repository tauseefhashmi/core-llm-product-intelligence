# Product research notes

## ZenBook 14
Independent reviews consistently describe the ZenBook 14 as a highly portable productivity laptop. Battery-life tests generally place it ahead of the average thin-and-light category. The 16GB configuration is suitable for analytics notebooks, browser-heavy workflows and office applications. It is not a strong choice for sustained GPU workloads.

## ThinkPad E14
The ThinkPad E14 is valued for keyboard quality, business-oriented durability and serviceability. Reviews often recommend it for engineering and office workloads. Battery life is good rather than exceptional. Upgradeability can be useful for users who expect to keep many development tools open simultaneously.

## Swift Go 14
The Swift Go 14 is positioned as a lower-cost portable option. Review feedback highlights good mobility and competitive battery efficiency for its price. The main compromise is a less premium build and fewer business-oriented management features.

## Studio Pro 16
The Studio Pro 16 targets creators and developers with GPU-heavy workloads. It has the strongest performance potential in this small catalog but costs significantly more and is currently on backorder.

## PowerBook X
The PowerBook X balances CPU performance and memory capacity. It is a better fit than ultraportables for local development environments that need more memory, but its price-to-portability ratio is weaker.
